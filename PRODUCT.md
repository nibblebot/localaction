# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web — desktop-first, with full mobile adaptation below 768px (drawer navigation, 44px touch targets under coarse pointer).

## Users

Solo power users: people who organize life and projects seriously — GTD practitioners and OmniFocus/Things refugees. One person at the keyboard, running their whole life through the app, often with deep area trees, many projects, and heavy keyboard use. The interface must stay fast and legible at that scale.

## Product Purpose

A calm, self-owned place for everything that matters. LocalAction organizes life by ongoing areas, bounded projects, and one-off tasks, with notes woven through — all offline-first, with data the user truly owns.

## Positioning

Local-first ownership paired with serious structural depth. No other tool gives you both a fully self-owned, offline-capable store (SQLite on disk, OPFS in browser, sync you run yourself) and a genuine GTD-depth model — areas, sub-areas, projects, sections, nested sub-tasks, notes. Every screen reinforces that pairing: your data, structured deeply.

## Brand Personality

Calm precision. Three words: composed, exact, unhurried. The reference is Things by Cultured Code — calm hierarchy, one-accent restraint, daily-driver polish. The surface stays quiet; the emotion it should leave after ten minutes is energized momentum — the app feels eager and responsive under the hand, never sleepy. Copy is plain and confident; decoration earns its place or is cut.

## Anti-references

- Legacy enterprise PM (Asana/Jira): feature walls, side-stripe banners, enterprise-gray heaviness.
- Consumer cutesy: playful gradients, over-rounded cards, mascot energy.
- Evernote-era clutter: maximalist chrome, information density without hierarchy.
- Gray spreadsheet: pure utilitarian gray with no personality at all.

## Design Principles

1. Local-first is visible, not hidden. Sync and persistence states are legible in the shell (the sidebar sync badge is canonical) — ownership is a feature users can see.
2. Areas-first hierarchy stays legible. The area tree is the mental model; rollups, counts, and breadcrumbs keep deep structure comprehensible at a glance.
3. Calm surface, alive under the hand. Restrained visuals — one accent, quiet surfaces — paired with snappy, immediate interaction feedback. Calm precision in how it looks; energized momentum in how it responds.
4. Depth through polish, not feature walls. Advanced capability (drag-anywhere trees, sub-tasks, sections, rollups) is demonstrated by craft and discoverability, never by stacking chrome.
5. The keyboard is a first-class surface. Every primary flow is reachable and readable without a pointer, with visible focus states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text at 4.5:1 contrast, large text at 3:1, against every themed surface (light and dark). Full keyboard navigation with preserved focus-visible states. Reduced-motion and density preferences respected.
