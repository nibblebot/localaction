# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web — desktop-first, with full mobile adaptation below 768px (drawer navigation, 44px touch targets under coarse pointer).

## Users

Solo power users running their whole life through the app — GTD practitioners and OmniFocus/Things refugees. Deep area trees, many projects, heavy keyboard use; the interface must stay fast and legible at that scale.

## Product Purpose

A calm, self-owned place for everything that matters: ongoing areas, bounded projects, and one-off tasks, with notes woven through — offline-first, with data the user truly owns.

## Positioning

Local-first ownership paired with serious structural depth: a fully self-owned, offline-capable store plus a genuine GTD-depth model (areas, sub-areas, projects, sections, nested sub-tasks, notes).

## Brand Personality

Calm precision: composed, exact, unhurried. The reference is Things by Cultured Code — calm hierarchy, one-accent restraint, daily-driver polish.

## Anti-references

See DESIGN.md §1.

## Design Principles

1. Local-first is visible: sync and persistence states are legible in the shell.
2. Areas-first hierarchy stays legible: rollups, counts, and breadcrumbs keep deep structure comprehensible.
3. Calm surface, alive under the hand: restrained visuals, immediate feedback.
4. Depth through polish, not feature walls: capability via craft, never stacked chrome.
5. Keyboard-first: every primary flow reachable without a pointer, with visible focus.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text, every themed surface). Full keyboard navigation with focus-visible states; reduced-motion and density preferences respected.
